## 3. “Currently, On The Market” weekly email

Verify the following:

- A reusable email template exists.
- The dynamic subject follows:
  `Currently, On The Market | {{week_start}}–{{week_end}}`
- The heading displays the same weekly date range.
- Date ranges format correctly across:
  - A normal week
  - Two different months
  - Two different years
- The email uses the correct Barefoot Bay local timezone.
- The email includes only listings first published within the target weekly period.
- Only active and publicly visible listings are included.
- Draft, expired, deleted, and unpublished listings are excluded.
- Listings appear newest first.
- Each listing includes:
  - Primary image or fallback image
  - Title
  - Price
  - Listing type
  - Short description
  - Working View Listing link
- The View All Listings button links to the public On The Market page.
- The Post a Listing button links to the correct listing-submission page.
- The email is responsive and readable on desktop and mobile.
- Image alt text is present.
- The email remains understandable when images are blocked.
- A plain-text version exists.
- The weekly scheduled job exists.
- An administrator can configure the send day and time.
- An administrator can:
  - Enable or disable automation
  - Preview the email
  - Send a test email
  - Review included listings
  - Manually trigger a campaign
  - Review send history and errors
- The same weekly campaign cannot be sent twice accidentally.
- Temporary retry behavior does not create duplicate campaigns.
- No email is sent when there are no qualifying listings unless that behavior is explicitly enabled.
- Only approved promotional-email subscribers receive the email.
- Unsubscribed, suppressed, and invalid recipients are excluded.
- Existing unsubscribe and email-preference behavior remains functional.
- The footer contains the required business identity, mailing information, and unsubscribe link.
- Email credentials and provider configuration are stored in environment variables.
- The scheduled automation remains disabled until the sender identity, subscriber source, schedule, and test delivery have been verified.
