Launch Concept: “Beach-to-Launch Reveal” (Updated)
One-liner

A sun-washed beach scene builds anticipation. You tap Launch, a mission-style countdown appears with playful comms, the rocket lifts off and pulls the landing screen away like a curtain, revealing the live Barefoot Bay homepage beneath.

Experience arc (8–12 seconds total)

Quiet shoreline
Golden-hour beach: gentle waves, soft clouds, warm sun. Everything feels relaxed and premium.

Rocket prep (idle)
A compact rocket with surfboard-style fins idles on a tiki pad at the bottom center—tiny smoke puffs, subtle bob, two little nav lights blinking out of sync.

Host taps “Launch” → countdown begins
The scene tightens. A bold T-10 countdown appears at the top with a slim “Mission Control” text strip beside it (or directly underneath on mobile). From T-10 to T-0, the mood escalates: smoke grows a touch thicker, a faint rumble enters, and the screen has a barely-there tremble near T-3.

Ignition beat
At T-1 → T-0, lights flicker once, the rumble swells for half a second, and we get a crisp, celebratory whoosh (sound is optional and off by default).

The pull-away reveal
A thin tow line appears above the rocket. As it lifts, the rocket yanks the entire landing screen upward like a banner. The live Barefoot Bay homepage is already loaded below, fully interactive.

Hand-off flourish
The rocket arcs to a corner and its contrail resolves into a gentle underline near the hero headline or navigation—like it “signed” the page.

Settle state
Ambient life remains subtle: a faint wave shimmer and slow cloud drift. “Replay launch” sits politely in a corner; “Skip intro” is always available.

Variant (if you prefer): Instead of pulling the screen, the launch smoke blooms into a soft haze that dissolves the landing layer and clears to reveal the live site underneath.

Mission-style copy next to the countdown

Use a clean, high-contrast “comms strip” beside the timer (or stacked on mobile). It should feel like friendly mission control—short lines, confident tone, little beach wink.

Option A — Classic mission vibe (with your line included)

T-10: “Barefoot Bay, we are clear for launch.”

T-7: “Go/No-Go poll complete—Barefoot Bay is go for launch.”

T-5: “Range green. Weather is a go.”

T-4: “Auto-sequence start.”

T-3: “Guidance is internal.”

T-2: “Main engine arm enabled.”

T-1: “Ignition sequence start.”

T-0: “Liftoff—Barefoot Bay is live!”

Option B — Beach-town flavor (playful but tight)

T-10: “Barefoot Bay, we are clear for launch.”

T-7: “Skies clear, surf mellow—range is green.”

T-5: “Boards stowed. Tiki pad secure.”

T-3: “Guidance locked. All hands barefoot.”

T-2: “Engines humming.”

T-1: “Spark on deck.”

T-0: “Liftoff! Welcome to Barefoot Bay.”

Presentation notes (non-technical):

Keep each line on screen for ~0.8–1.2s; let it advance with the timer.

Add a tiny “comms chirp” or soft type-on effect for personality (still readable from across a room).

Make “Barefoot Bay, we are clear for launch.” the first line after the button click so it feels like the official call.

Copy & on-screen elements

Primary CTA: “Launch” (starts the countdown).

Secondary: “Skip intro” (always visible), “Replay” (after reveal).

Post-reveal headline: “Barefoot Bay is live.” or “Welcome to Barefoot Bay.”

Accessibility courtesy: If a visitor prefers reduced motion, skip the animation entirely; offer a “Play animation” button.

Art direction & tone (quick)

Warm beach palette (seafoam, sky, sand, coral). Soft gradients with a touch of texture so it feels human, not sterile. “Retro NASA meets beach town”—optimistic, social, welcoming.

Party-mode extras (optional)

Host control: Park on the pre-launch scene before the event, then tap Launch live for the room.

Projector loop: After reveal, auto-scroll a few highlight sections, then gracefully reset to the beach pre-launch state.

Light celebratory confetti: A handful of shells or starfish sparkle for one second at reveal—minimal and classy.

Acceptance checklist (for the team)

Countdown begins when Launch is clicked and includes the comms strip.

The line “Barefoot Bay, we are clear for launch.” appears right after the click.

The reveal (pull-away or smoke dissolve) feels unmistakable and delightful.

Live homepage is fully interactive the instant it appears.

Skip + Replay + Sound toggle are present; experience holds up with sound off and with reduced motion
